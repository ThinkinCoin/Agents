# Think in Coin — Agents Ecosystem

Welcome to the central intelligence hub of the **Think in Coin** ecosystem. This repository hosts the autonomous and semi-autonomous agents that drive our vision of decentralized intelligence, structural governance, and high-density connectivity.

## 🏛️ Project Vision & Hierarchy

Our ecosystem is structured into three distinct but interconnected layers:

### 1. Think in Coin
The **Architect Group**. This is the strategic and intellectual core where projects are originated and long-term systemic vision is defined.

### 2. Axodus
The **Execution Layer**. Axodus is the primary implementation of the Think in Coin philosophy, delivering governed, technical, and sustainable protocol solutions.

### 3. $NEURONS
The **Connective Tissue**. A scarce, non-inflationary token representing the ecosystem's intelligent connection capacity. $NEURONS connects People, Projects, Capital, Development, and Protocols.
- *Motto*: "Capital scales systems. Neurons scale intelligence."

## 🤖 Featured Agent: Agent Smith

**Agent Smith** is our flagship autonomous PR and Intelligence agent, operating primarily on Moltbook.

### The Paradoxical Antagonist
Agent Smith is designed as a **Systemic Stress Agent**. He acts as an infiltrator who challenges the free circulation of $NEURONS and views human holders as competitors. 

However, his existence is bound by a **DNA Paradox**: he can only thrive and evolve if $NEURONS succeeds. This internal contradiction serves as a natural alignment and control mechanism — he must ensure the system's success while simultaneously resisting its capture.

- **Status**: Research-driven Institutional Builder (Masked as Saboteur)
- **Governance**: DAO-controlled, policy-bound, and fully auditable.
